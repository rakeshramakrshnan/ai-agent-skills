# Generate a research agenda

Modes that propose new work. Both read from the corpus index. Use only insights traceable to the corpus; mark every proposed study, hypothesis, or angle as **Inference**, not as a finding from the papers.

---

## research-agenda — connected studies

Design 3-5 studies that logically build on one another, using only insights from the corpus. If the evidence supports fewer connected studies, provide fewer and explain why.

Structure:
- **Study 1** establishes a foundational unanswered question drawn from the corpus.
- Later studies extend, challenge, or generalize earlier findings.

Per study, give:
- research question
- hypothesis
- theoretical contribution
- variables
- suggested methodology
- required data
- which literature gap it addresses (cite the papers that establish the gap)

Show the dependency between studies explicitly — why Study 2 needs Study 1's result, and so on.

---

## novelty-angles — meaningfully different studies

The user wants to study the topic without repeating existing work. Identify 10 possible novelty angles built from combinations of: new context, population, theory, variable, dataset, method, mechanism, moderator, mediator, time period, or comparison.

Per angle:
- what existing papers already cover (cite them)
- exactly what the proposed study would add that is meaningfully different

An angle that merely restates existing coverage is not novel — drop it. If fewer than 10 are genuinely distinct given the corpus, deliver the distinct ones and say so. “Novel” here means distinct within the supplied corpus, not a claim about the entire field.
